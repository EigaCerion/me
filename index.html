About

Started in IT support. Building software now.

I'm a recent Information Systems graduate (Bina Sarana Informatika University, GPA 3.71/4.00) who spent a year in the trenches of IT support before turning toward software development full-time. As an IT & Facility Building intern at PT. Angkasa Pura II, I resolved 150+ technical incident tickets, supported 200+ users across hardware, software, and network issues, and cut average resolution time by 20% — mostly by actually documenting root causes instead of just closing tickets.

That experience taught me to think in systems before I could write much code: how a request actually flows, what breaks under real usage, and why documentation and clear error messages save everyone time later. I've since been applying that instinct to full-stack projects of my own — most recently a self-hosted comic reader with a real backend, database, and image-compression pipeline (see Mission: Archive below).

I'm PCAP-certified in Python (Cisco Networking Academy) and a BNSP-Certified Software Developer, and I'm actively looking for a software engineering role where I can keep growing.
